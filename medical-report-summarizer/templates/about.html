Architecture & NLP Pipeline

How MedSum works

Every uploaded medical report passes through an eight-stage hybrid NLP pipeline designed for clinical accuracy, fact verification, and patient readability.

  1. Stage 01 · Input
    Upload Accepts digital text files, multi-page PDFs, or scanned photos of hospital reports.
  2. Stage 02 · OCR
    Text extraction PyMuPDF parses native PDFs, while Tesseract OCR extracts text from scanned images after grayscale, contrast, and threshold clean-up.
  3. Stage 03 · Cleaning
    Pre-processing Strips page headers and numbering noise, expands clinical abbreviations (e.g., HTN → hypertension), detects report sections, and segments sentences.
  4. Stage 04 · Extraction
    Medical NER & lab parsing A clinical lexicon with negation detection—plus an optional biomedical BERT model (d4data/biomedical-ner-all)—tags diseases, symptoms, drugs, and procedures while benchmarking lab values against reference ranges.
  5. Stage 05 · Generation
    Summarization Extractive: TextRank (TF-IDF + cosine similarity + PageRank) with medical boosts. Abstractive: BART transformer ({{ model.split('/')[-1] }}). Hybrid: TextRank filters key sentences and BART rewrites them.
  6. Stage 06 · Guardrail
    Fact check Cross-verifies every numerical lab value and medical term in the summary against the source document to catch hallucinations and measure key-fact coverage.
  7. Stage 07 · Clinical View
    Doctor summary Generates a concise clinical overview with out-of-range vitals and abnormal lab biomarkers highlighted for rapid review.
  8. Stage 08 · Plain Language
    Patient summary Translates medical jargon into everyday language using a curated glossary, adds context for abnormal results, and scores clarity with the Flesch Reading Ease metric.

TextRank scoring

Graph Ranking

WS(Vi) = (1 − d) + d · Σj [ wji / Σk wjk ] · WS(Vj)

Here, d = 0.85 is the damping factor and w represents cosine similarity between sentence TF-IDF vectors. Each sentence score is further weighted by section importance (Diagnosis and Impression rank highest), medical entity density, and abnormal-lab boosts.
